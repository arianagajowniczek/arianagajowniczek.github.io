# NOTES: design decisions and changelog

## Decisions (approved by Ariana, Nov 6, 2026)
- **Audience:** recruiters and employers. Light, neutral, professional; easy to read.
- **Idea:** the Home summary is laid out like a bond term sheet (label/value rows), a nod to the
  Treasury bond financing meetings that started the industry project.
- **Palette:** ground #F6F6F3, surface #E7E5E0, ink #1F2329, muted #5E6670, accent navy #1E3A5F.
- **Type:** Source Serif 4 (headings), Public Sans (body; the U.S. government's typeface).
- **Interactive piece:** "Where my experience fits" uses native `<details>`, no JavaScript.
- **Content sources:** only files in the 490AI folder (résumé, industry_interview.md). Nothing else.
- **Writing:** headline and Home intro written on request (intro from her cover-letter wording); other prose left as [PLACEHOLDER] (CLAUDE.md rule).
- **Privacy:** no phone, home address, student ID, GPA or transcript. Résumé PDF not copied in (it has phone and GPA).
